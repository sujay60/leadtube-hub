🔍 How It Works

  • 1.
    Proxy Scraping: The tool uses our backend agent to safely fetch the creator's YouTube page to retrieve their custom bio, About description, and embedded social links.
  • 2.
    Multi-Signal AI Extraction: It feeds all harvested variables (channel name, links, description, emails, handles) into Gemini AI to isolate the host's name.
  • 3.
    Enriched Export: Appends a dedicated Host Name column to your original CSV layout and makes it immediately downloadable.

💡 Pro Tips

  • •
    Make sure your CSV columns are titled clearly (e.g. Channel Name, Link or Channel Link, Emails).
  • •
    Email handles (e.g. rubygranger@...) are crucial signals utilized by Gemini to resolve first and last names.
  • •
    Churches, companies, or collective channels without an active individual host are labeled as Brand or Organization automatically.
